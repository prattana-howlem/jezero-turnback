    Simulation assumptions

    A 90-minute mission includes a 15-minute return reserve. Flat-ground pace is 0.7 m/s. Travel time increases 3.5% per degree of sampled slope. Routes avoid sampled slopes above 25°. The reach area estimates a walk from the crew’s current position to each point and back to the habitat. Science task time is included in the mission plan. The habitat, walking model, science tasks and delay are simulated.
